# Datashard

**Your company's verified knowledge, inside your AI.**

Datashard gives your AI assistant your company's checked facts, and only the ones each person is allowed to see. It works with Claude, Codex and Grok Bot, and runs in your own Cloudflare account.

- **Verified facts:** every fact has a named owner and records who checked it and when.
- **Role-based access:** sections are granted to roles through Google Workspace, Okta or Microsoft Entra ID groups.
- **Your own install:** your own sign-in and storage, with no shared database.
- **Search tested on every deploy:** search is checked against real questions before each release.
